### D4-001

`Skill 4.1.2` · Multiple choice

A company has a dozen isolated generative AI experiments spread across its departments, with no shared strategy, no accountable owners, and no success metrics. The CEO wants the company to be "AI-first within a year" and asks whether to build a full enterprise AI platform now. What is the best next step?

- **A.** Build the full enterprise platform now, so that every department can move to it immediately.
- **B.** Stop all current experiments until the enterprise platform is ready.
- **C.** Define an AI strategy tied to business goals, assign owners, and select a few pilots with success metrics to reach the next maturity stage.
- **D.** Hire a large data science team to take over all of the experiments centrally.

<details><summary>Resposta</summary>

**C.** A empresa está na experimentação. O passo certo é o próximo nível (estratégia, donos, pilotos com métrica), não saltar para a escala corporativa.

- A: plataforma sem estratégia nem casos priorizados vira custo sem uso.
- B: joga fora aprendizado e engajamento.
- D: centralizar tudo não resolve a falta de estratégia e de donos de negócio.

</details>

### D4-002

`Skill 4.2.1` · Multiple choice

A retailer's highest-priority AI use case, personalized offers, depends on customer data that is spread across five systems with inconsistent customer IDs. Leadership is eager to start building the model. Where should the next investment go?

- **A.** Integrating and governing the customer data that the use case requires.
- **B.** Selecting the most advanced foundation model available.
- **C.** Running company-wide hackathons to find more use cases.
- **D.** Buying seat-based licenses for the marketing team.

<details><summary>Resposta</summary>

**A.** A lacuna que bloqueia o caso de maior valor vem primeiro. Dado em silo, sem chave comum, impede o caso de funcionar, com qualquer modelo.

- B: o modelo não compensa dado fragmentado.
- C: mais casos de uso não destravam o prioritário.
- D: licença não resolve dado.

</details>

### D4-003

`Skill 4.3.3` · Multiple choice

Employees in a claims-processing department are resisting a new AI tool, and rumors of layoffs are spreading. What should leadership do?

- **A.** Make use of the tool mandatory and track each employee's compliance.
- **B.** Wait until the rollout is complete before communicating about the impact on jobs.
- **C.** Limit communication to the technical benefits of the tool to avoid discussing roles.
- **D.** Communicate openly about what will change, how roles will evolve, and what reskilling support will be offered, and involve employees in shaping the new workflow.

<details><summary>Resposta</summary>

**D.** Comunicação transparente, em mão dupla, e envolvimento das pessoas no desenho reduzem a resistência. O silêncio alimenta o boato.

- A: imposição aumenta a resistência e gera uso de fachada.
- B e C: evitar o tema do emprego confirma o medo.

</details>

### D4-004

`Skill 4.4.3` · Multiple choice

A company's AI Center of Excellence (CoE) is required to build every AI use case itself. Its backlog is now 14 months long, and business units have started to work around it. Which operating model should the company move to?

- **A.** Disband the CoE and let each business unit set its own AI standards.
- **B.** A hub-and-spoke model, in which the CoE provides standards, guardrails, reusable components, and enablement while the business units deliver their own use cases.
- **C.** Double the CoE's headcount so that it can clear the backlog faster.
- **D.** Freeze new use case requests until the CoE clears its current backlog.

<details><summary>Resposta</summary>

**B.** O CoE habilita e dá padrão; as áreas executam. Hub-and-spoke equilibra controle e velocidade.

- A: perde padrão, governança e reaproveitamento.
- C: mantém o gargalo, só mais caro.
- D: trava o valor e aumenta o contorno.

</details>

### D4-005

`Skill 4.4.5` · Multiple response

A successful AI pilot in one region has been stuck for nine months without moving to production. Which TWO factors are the most likely causes? (Select TWO.)

- **A.** The pilot used a foundation model rather than a custom-trained model.
- **B.** No business owner was accountable for production results or had an operating budget.
- **C.** The pilot measured a performance baseline before it started.
- **D.** Integration with the real business process and production systems was never planned.
- **E.** The pilot had an executive sponsor who approved its original scope.

<details><summary>Resposta</summary>

**B e D.** Pilot purgatory costuma vir de falta de dono e orçamento para operar, e de um piloto que nunca foi desenhado para entrar no processo real.

- A: usar FM não impede produção; é o caminho comum.
- C e E: baseline e patrocinador são fatores de sucesso, não causas do problema.

</details>

### D4-006

`Skill 4.4.2` · Multiple choice

An organization is choosing its first AI initiative. Which option best follows the principle of starting with short-term wins?

- **A.** An internal assistant that drafts replies to frequent supplier inquiries, using available data, with human review and measurable time savings.
- **B.** Fully autonomous AI pricing across the entire product catalog.
- **C.** A two-year program to rebuild core systems around AI before launching any use case.
- **D.** AI-driven clinical triage for the company's employee health program.

<details><summary>Resposta</summary>

**A.** Ganho rápido: valor visível, risco baixo, dados disponíveis e revisão humana. Cria credibilidade e aprendizado para os casos maiores.

- B e D: alto risco para uma primeira iniciativa.
- C: dois anos sem entregar valor mina o patrocínio.

</details>

### D4-007

`Skill 4.3.1` · Multiple choice

Adoption of an approved AI assistant varies widely: some teams use it every day, while others barely use it at all. What is a low-cost way to spread adoption from colleague to colleague?

- **A.** Send a monthly reminder email from the IT department to every employee.
- **B.** Make usage mandatory and add an individual usage target to every employee's goals.
- **C.** Identify and support AI champions in each team who share use cases, help their colleagues, and pass feedback to the program.
- **D.** Remove the assistant from teams with low adoption to reduce license costs.

<details><summary>Resposta</summary>

**C.** AI champions espalham a adoção de colega para colega e trazem feedback das áreas.

- A: comunicação genérica, de mão única.
- B: meta de uso gera uso de fachada, não valor.
- D: corta custo mas desiste do valor, sem entender a causa da baixa adoção.

</details>

### D4-008

`Skill 4.3.6` · Multiple choice

In accounts payable, AI will soon extract and match about 85% of invoices automatically. How should the clerks' roles be redesigned?

- **A.** Eliminate all clerk positions as soon as the AI system goes live.
- **B.** Keep the clerks manually reviewing every invoice, exactly as they do today.
- **C.** Reassign the clerks to maintaining the AI model's code.
- **D.** Move the clerks to handling exceptions, validating AI outputs, and improving the process, with training to support the change.

<details><summary>Resposta</summary>

**D.** Transição de papel para supervisão humana: as pessoas tratam exceções e validam, somando julgamento e contexto à escala da AI. Com treinamento junto.

- A: perde a supervisão e o conhecimento do processo, justamente para os 15% difíceis.
- B: anula o ganho.
- C: manutenção de modelo é trabalho técnico, fora do perfil e do escopo.

</details>

### D4-009

`Skill 4.4.1` · Multiple choice

A company has identified and prioritized AI opportunities tied to business outcomes, and it has validated two of them with small proofs of concept. Following an iterative envision, experiment, launch, and scale approach, what should come next?

- **A.** Return to the envision phase to generate a longer list of opportunities.
- **B.** Launch the validated use cases as production pilots that deliver measurable business value.
- **C.** Scale both use cases across the entire enterprise immediately.
- **D.** Wait until the company reaches the highest level of its AI maturity model.

<details><summary>Resposta</summary>

**B.** Envision e experiment feitos; a próxima fase é launch, em produção e com valor medido. Scale vem depois.

- A: volta sem necessidade.
- C: pula o launch.
- D: a maturidade se ganha fazendo, não esperando.

</details>

### D4-010

`Skill 4.3.5` · Multiple choice

A company with 10,000 employees wants to build broad AI capability while reducing the risk of misuse. Which workforce development program is most appropriate?

- **A.** Role-based learning paths for executives, managers, end users, and builders, responsible AI training for everyone, and hackathons that surface use cases.
- **B.** Advanced training for the data science team only, since they build the AI solutions.
- **C.** A single mandatory video about AI that every employee watches once.
- **D.** Encouraging employees to learn on their own using public AI tools.

<details><summary>Resposta</summary>

**A.** Treinamento por perfil, RAI para todos e aprendizado prático (hackathons, POCs) são os elementos do guia.

- B: ignora quem usa e quem decide.
- C: raso e sem continuidade.
- D: alimenta shadow AI e não ensina uso responsável.

</details>

### D4-011

`Skill 4.1.1` · Multiple choice

A readiness assessment finds that a company has strong cloud infrastructure and high-quality, well-governed data. However, the executive team disagrees about whether AI should focus on cutting costs or on driving growth, and no one owns the AI budget. Which readiness dimension is the main gap?

- **A.** Infrastructure
- **B.** Data quality
- **C.** Leadership alignment
- **D.** Governance

<details><summary>Resposta</summary>

**C.** Desacordo sobre o objetivo e ausência de dono do orçamento são falta de alinhamento da liderança. Sem isso, a prontidão técnica não vira resultado.

- A e B: o diagnóstico diz que estão fortes.
- D: governança trata de políticas e risco; o problema descrito é de direção.

</details>

### D4-012

`Skill 4.4.6` · Multiple choice

A company is expanding an AI customer assistant from one country to twelve. What is the best way to protect business continuity and performance during the expansion?

- **A.** Launch in all twelve countries on the same day to shorten the transition.
- **B.** Roll out in phases by country, monitoring performance and cost, with a human fallback and a rollback plan.
- **C.** Pause the assistant in the first country until all twelve are ready to launch together.
- **D.** Let each country's team configure and launch its own version without central monitoring.

<details><summary>Resposta</summary>

**B.** Rollout gradual com monitoramento, fallback e rollback protege continuidade e desempenho ao escalar.

- A: concentra o risco num único dia.
- C: interrompe um valor que já existe.
- D: sem padrão e sem monitoramento, os problemas aparecem tarde.

</details>
