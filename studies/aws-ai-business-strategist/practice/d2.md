### D2-001

`Skill 2.2.2` · Multiple choice

A company ran a three-month pilot of a generative AI assistant for its customer service agents. Leadership now asks for proof of business value. The team can show usage statistics and positive comments from agents, but it cannot demonstrate any improvement in average handle time or first-contact resolution. What was most likely missing from the pilot?

- **A.** A larger foundation model with a longer context window.
- **B.** A seat-based license so that every agent had access.
- **C.** Chain-of-thought prompting to improve answer quality.
- **D.** Baseline measurements of handle time and resolution rates taken before the pilot began.

<details><summary>Resposta</summary>

**D.** Sem baseline medido antes, não existe "antes e depois" para provar ganho. Medir o baseline é o primeiro passo de qualquer plano de medição.

- A e C: mudanças técnicas não criam, depois do fato, uma comparação que não foi medida.
- B: o modelo de licença não tem relação com a prova de valor.

</details>

### D2-002

`Skill 2.2.3` · Multiple choice

In the first year, an AI document-processing solution costs USD 250,000 in licenses and infrastructure, USD 100,000 in systems integration, and USD 50,000 in employee training. It delivers USD 600,000 in productivity gains. What is the first-year ROI?

- **A.** 140%
- **B.** 71%
- **C.** 50%
- **D.** 33%

<details><summary>Resposta</summary>

**C.** ROI = (benefícios − custos) ÷ custos × 100. Custos totais = 250 + 100 + 50 = 400 mil. (600 − 400) ÷ 400 × 100 = **50%**.

- A: considera só licença e infraestrutura como custo, ignorando os custos "ocultos" de integração e treinamento.
- B: esquece o treinamento.
- D: divide pelo benefício em vez do custo.

</details>

### D2-003

`Skill 2.2.4` · Multiple choice

Four weeks into the rollout of an AI writing assistant to 2,000 sales representatives, the CFO wants an early signal of whether the investment will pay off, well before quarterly revenue results are available. Which metric is the best leading indicator?

- **A.** Weekly active usage, and the share of AI-generated drafts that representatives send with only minor edits.
- **B.** Quarterly revenue per sales representative.
- **C.** The model's score on a public language benchmark.
- **D.** Year-over-year change in customer lifetime value.

<details><summary>Resposta</summary>

**A.** Adoção e taxa de aceitação das sugestões são leading indicators: mostram cedo se o valor vai aparecer.

- B e D: lagging indicators, que só se movem depois de meses.
- C: métrica técnica, não indica valor de negócio.

</details>

### D2-004

`Skill 2.1.2` · Multiple choice

A mid-sized manufacturer wants AI to summarize meetings and draft routine emails for its office staff. The need is common across industries, does not differentiate the company from its competitors, and must deliver value within weeks. The IT team is small. Which approach is most appropriate?

- **A.** Build a custom model on Amazon SageMaker AI trained on the company's internal emails.
- **B.** Buy an existing packaged solution that meets the company's security and data-use requirements.
- **C.** Partner with a university to research new summarization techniques.
- **D.** Build and host an in-house large language model to keep full control of the technology.

<details><summary>Resposta</summary>

**B.** Necessidade comum, sem diferencial, prazo curto e time pequeno: buy. A diligência que importa é a de segurança e de uso de dados pelo fornecedor.

- A e D: build faz sentido para diferencial competitivo com dados proprietários, e aqui não há nenhum dos dois.
- C: pesquisa não entrega valor em semanas.

</details>

### D2-005

`Skill 2.1.3` · Multiple choice

During a quarterly AI portfolio review: Pilot 1 met all of its value targets. Pilot 2 shows strong results in testing but cannot go further until the customer data it needs is integrated, which the data team will finish next quarter. Pilot 3 has run for nine months with low adoption and no realistic path to its value targets. What decisions should the review make?

- **A.** Scale all three pilots to maintain momentum and demonstrate commitment to AI.
- **B.** Scale Pilot 1, and terminate Pilots 2 and 3 to focus resources.
- **C.** Scale Pilot 1, pause Pilot 2 until the data integration is complete, and terminate Pilot 3.
- **D.** Pause all three pilots until a full governance review of the portfolio is complete.

<details><summary>Resposta</summary>

**C.** As três saídas do guia: escalar o que bateu meta, pausar o que tem valor mas está bloqueado, encerrar o que não tem caminho para o valor.

- A: escalar o Pilot 3 desperdiça recurso.
- B: encerrar o Pilot 2 joga fora um valor comprovado por um bloqueio temporário.
- D: parar o que funciona não tem justificativa.

</details>

### D2-006

`Skill 2.1.4` · Multiple choice

The COO lists four candidate initiatives for the AI program. For which one should the AI strategist advise against using AI?

- **A.** Summarizing thousands of free-text support tickets each week to find recurring problems.
- **B.** Extracting fields from invoices sent in hundreds of different supplier layouts.
- **C.** Drafting first replies to customer emails for agents to review before sending.
- **D.** Calculating payroll tax deductions with formulas that are fully defined by law.

<details><summary>Resposta</summary>

**D.** Fórmula fixa, definida em lei, exigindo resultado exato e auditável: é trabalho para regra determinística. AI adicionaria custo e risco de erro.

- A, B e C: texto não estruturado, variedade de formatos e rascunho com revisão humana são bons usos de AI.

</details>

### D2-007

`Skill 2.2.5` · Multiple choice

A retailer is launching a generative AI shopping assistant. Traffic is hard to predict and is expected to vary widely with seasonal campaigns. Finance wants costs during the pilot to follow actual usage, without paying for idle capacity. Which pricing model fits best?

- **A.** Consumption-based pricing charged per request and per token, such as on-demand use of Amazon Bedrock.
- **B.** Instance-based pricing, with capacity reserved for the expected peak season.
- **C.** Seat-based licenses for every employee in the marketing department.
- **D.** A three-year committed capacity agreement negotiated before the pilot starts.

<details><summary>Resposta</summary>

**A.** Uso imprevisível em piloto pede custo que acompanha o uso. Consumption-based "escala com o sucesso" e não cobra ociosidade.

- B: instance-based custa o mesmo parado; reservar para o pico paga a ociosidade o ano inteiro.
- C: seat-based cobra por pessoa, e o usuário aqui é o cliente final.
- D: compromisso longo antes de conhecer o padrão de uso é o oposto do pedido.

</details>

### D2-008

`Skill 2.3.3` · Multiple choice

A retailer's board asks which AI investment is most likely to create a competitive advantage that rivals cannot quickly copy. What should the AI strategist recommend?

- **A.** Being the first retailer in the market to adopt the newest publicly available foundation model.
- **B.** Combining proprietary customer and supply chain data with AI embedded in core merchandising decisions.
- **C.** Licensing the same off-the-shelf chatbot that the current market leader uses.
- **D.** Running a large advertising campaign that highlights the company's use of AI.

<details><summary>Resposta</summary>

**B.** Foundation models estão disponíveis para todos. Difícil de copiar é o dado proprietário integrado ao processo central do negócio, que ainda melhora com o uso.

- A: vantagem temporária; o concorrente adota o mesmo modelo em semanas.
- C: por definição, é paridade, não vantagem.
- D: marketing não é capacidade.

</details>

### D2-009

`Skill 2.2.1` · Multiple response

A business case for an AI invoice-processing solution lists several expected benefits. Which TWO are intangible benefits? (Select TWO.)

- **A.** A reduction of 120 staff hours per month spent on manual data entry.
- **B.** Higher engagement among finance employees, who spend less time on repetitive work.
- **C.** USD 80,000 per year in avoided overtime payments.
- **D.** A 15% reduction in the cost of late-payment penalties.
- **E.** Greater organizational agility in closing the books when business conditions change.

<details><summary>Resposta</summary>

**B e E.** Engajamento e agilidade são benefícios intangíveis: entram no business case, mas não viram valor em dinheiro direto.

- A, C e D: horas, economia de horas extras e redução de multas são tangíveis e mensuráveis em dinheiro.

</details>

### D2-010

`Skill 2.1.5` · Multiple choice

A company will replace manual review of supplier invoices in accounts payable with AI-based extraction and matching. The month-end close cannot be put at risk. What is the best transition approach?

- **A.** Switch off the manual process on the go-live date so that employees are forced to adopt the new system.
- **B.** Wait to launch until the extraction model reaches 100% accuracy in testing.
- **C.** Let each accounts payable clerk decide whether to use the AI system or the manual process.
- **D.** Run both processes in parallel for a period, compare results against the baseline, keep the manual process as a fallback, and phase it out as performance is proven.

<details><summary>Resposta</summary>

**D.** Transição com continuidade do negócio: paralelo, comparação com baseline, fallback e desligamento gradual.

- A: arrisca justamente o fechamento que não pode falhar.
- B: 100% de acurácia é meta irreal; nenhuma solução chega lá.
- C: sem critério, não há medição nem padrão de processo.

</details>

### D2-011

`Skill 2.3.4` · Multiple choice

A regional insurer learns that most of its competitors have already deployed AI-assisted claims triage, and customers now expect same-day claim decisions. How should leadership frame its AI investment?

- **A.** As an optional innovation experiment, funded with a small exploratory budget.
- **B.** As a first-mover bet on emerging technology that the market has not yet validated.
- **C.** As a competitive necessity to close a gap with the market, prioritizing proven solutions that can be deployed quickly.
- **D.** As a later priority, to be revisited once the insurance market reaches a higher level of AI maturity.

<details><summary>Resposta</summary>

**C.** Num setor onde a adoção já é alta, AI deixou de ser diferencial e virou requisito para competir. O investimento defende a posição, com soluções comprovadas e rápidas.

- A: subestima um risco competitivo real.
- B: o mercado já validou; não há first move a fazer.
- D: o mercado já está maduro; esperar amplia a distância.

</details>
