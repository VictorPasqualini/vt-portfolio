### D3-001

`Skill 3.1.4` · Multiple choice

A bank is introducing an AI model that recommends whether to approve or decline personal loan applications. Regulators require that decisions with significant effects on individuals be reviewed by a person and that applicants receive a meaningful explanation. Which oversight model should the bank adopt?

- **A.** Human-out-of-the-loop, so that every application is decided consistently by the same model.
- **B.** Human-on-the-loop, with a risk analyst reviewing a monthly sample of decisions.
- **C.** Human-in-the-loop, with a credit officer reviewing and approving each recommendation before it takes effect.
- **D.** Fully automated decisions, with an appeal process available to declined applicants afterward.

<details><summary>Resposta</summary>

**C.** Decisão de alto impacto sobre pessoas, com exigência regulatória de revisão: human-in-the-loop, com aprovação humana antes do efeito.

- A e D: automação total contraria a exigência; apelação depois não substitui revisão antes.
- B: amostra mensal é supervisão, não revisão de cada decisão.

</details>

### D3-002

`Skill 3.1.1` · Multiple choice

An online retailer adds a notice to its website stating that product recommendations are generated by AI, describing which categories of customer data are used, and explaining how customers can opt out. Which responsible AI principle does this primarily address?

- **A.** Transparency
- **B.** Explainability
- **C.** Robustness
- **D.** Fairness

<details><summary>Resposta</summary>

**A.** Informar que existe AI, com quais dados e como sair, é transparency: dar às pessoas informação para decidirem sobre o uso.

- B: explainability seria explicar *por que* uma recomendação específica foi feita.
- C: robustness é funcionar bem com entradas inesperadas.
- D: fairness trata de resultados injustos entre grupos.

</details>

### D3-003

`Skill 3.3.2` · Multiple choice

A company's AI résumé-screening model passed a fairness assessment before launch. A year later, the company has expanded into new regions and its applicant pool has changed significantly. What should AI governance require?

- **A.** No further action, because the model's fairness was validated before launch.
- **B.** Removal of all demographic information from the logs to eliminate the possibility of bias.
- **C.** Replacement of the model with a larger model trained on more data.
- **D.** Ongoing monitoring of outcomes across applicant groups to detect bias drift, with thresholds that trigger a review.

<details><summary>Resposta</summary>

**D.** Um modelo justo no lançamento pode ficar enviesado quando a população muda: bias drift. A checagem de viés é contínua.

- A: validação única ignora a mudança do público.
- B: sem os dados de grupo, fica impossível medir disparidade; o viés continua, só fica invisível.
- C: modelo maior não corrige viés por si só.

</details>

### D3-004

`Skill 3.2.4` · Multiple choice

A company requires an eight-week governance committee review for every AI use case, including low-risk ones such as summarizing internal meetings. Teams have started to bypass the process entirely. What is the best improvement?

- **A.** Add more members to the committee so that reviews can be completed faster.
- **B.** Adopt a risk classification framework, with a lightweight, fast approval path for low-risk use cases and full review for high-risk ones.
- **C.** Dissolve the committee and let each team decide which use cases need review.
- **D.** Require every team to self-certify all use cases, including high-risk ones.

<details><summary>Resposta</summary>

**B.** Controles proporcionais ao risco: governança rápida para o baixo risco, profunda para o alto. Assim o processo deixa de ser gargalo e para de ser contornado.

- A: mais gente no mesmo processo não muda o desenho.
- C e D: tiram a revisão justamente de onde ela é indispensável.

</details>

### D3-005

`Skill 3.2.1` · Multiple choice

After a customer-facing AI chatbot gave customers incorrect refund information, the legal, IT, data science, and customer service teams each said that another team was responsible. What governance element was missing?

- **A.** A named business owner accountable for each AI system, with roles and responsibilities defined across the cross-functional teams.
- **B.** A more capable foundation model with a lower error rate.
- **C.** A larger volume of training data about refund policies.
- **D.** A separate chatbot for each department involved in refunds.

<details><summary>Resposta</summary>

**A.** Governança multidisciplinar só funciona com accountability clara: um dono de negócio por sistema e papéis definidos (uma matriz RACI, por exemplo).

- B e C: melhoram a resposta, mas não resolvem quem responde quando algo dá errado.
- D: multiplica o problema.

</details>

### D3-006

`Skill 3.3.3` · Multiple response

A healthcare provider is launching a generative AI assistant to answer patients' questions about appointments and billing. The assistant must never offer medical diagnoses and must never reveal information about other patients. Which TWO controls best address these requirements? (Select TWO.)

- **A.** Use a larger model with more medical knowledge so that its answers are more accurate.
- **B.** Fine-tune the model on medical textbooks so that it understands clinical questions.
- **C.** Configure denied topics that block diagnosis requests and direct the patient to a clinician.
- **D.** Remove the option to escalate to human staff to reduce operating costs.
- **E.** Apply PII filtering and enforce access controls so that answers only use the requesting patient's own records.

<details><summary>Resposta</summary>

**C e E.** Denied topics impedem o tema proibido; filtro de PII e controle de acesso impedem a exposição de dados de outros pacientes. Ambos existem nos Bedrock Guardrails e no desenho de acesso da aplicação.

- A e B: tornar o modelo "mais médico" vai no sentido oposto ao requisito.
- D: escalonamento para humanos é uma salvaguarda; removê-lo aumenta o risco.

</details>

### D3-007

`Skill 3.2.3` · Multiple choice

A company is building a generative AI application on Amazon Bedrock. Under the AWS shared responsibility model, which responsibility stays with the company rather than with AWS?

- **A.** Physical security of the data centers where the models run.
- **B.** Patching the infrastructure that hosts the foundation models.
- **C.** Availability of the underlying global network.
- **D.** Controlling who can access the application's data and configuring guardrails suited to the use case.

<details><summary>Resposta</summary>

**D.** A AWS protege a infraestrutura e os serviços. O cliente responde pelos próprios dados, pelo controle de acesso, pela configuração dos guardrails e pelo uso que faz das saídas.

- A, B e C: são responsabilidades da AWS (segurança *da* nuvem).

</details>

### D3-008

`Skill 3.3.4` · Multiple choice

An internal HR assistant sometimes answers questions with policy details that sound plausible but do not exist. What is the most appropriate mitigation?

- **A.** Fine-tune the model on past employee chat conversations with the HR team.
- **B.** Ground answers in the official HR policy documents with RAG, require source citations, and apply contextual grounding checks.
- **C.** Increase the maximum length of responses so that answers include more detail.
- **D.** Switch off the assistant permanently and return to email-based HR support.

<details><summary>Resposta</summary>

**B.** Alucinação se reduz ancorando a resposta em fontes confiáveis, exigindo citação e checando o grounding.

- A: conversas antigas podem conter erros e não garantem fidelidade à política vigente.
- C: resposta mais longa tende a ter mais conteúdo inventado.
- D: desproporcional; há mitigação conhecida.

</details>

### D3-009

`Skill 3.2.2` · Multiple choice

A company offers a customer service chatbot to users in the European Union. Under a risk-based regulation such as the EU AI Act, which obligation most typically applies to a general customer service chatbot?

- **A.** The chatbot is prohibited and must be withdrawn from the EU market.
- **B.** The chatbot must be approved by a regulator before each new release.
- **C.** Users must be informed that they are interacting with an AI system.
- **D.** No obligations apply, because chatbots are outside the scope of AI regulation.

<details><summary>Resposta</summary>

**C.** Chatbot de atendimento geral fica, em regra, no risco limitado, cuja obrigação principal é de transparência: avisar que é uma AI.

- A: proibição é para risco inaceitável, como social scoring.
- B: não existe aprovação por release; alto risco exige avaliação de conformidade, não é o caso.
- D: chatbots estão no escopo, com obrigação de transparência.

</details>

### D3-010

`Skill 3.1.3` · Multiple choice

A product team plans to launch a generative AI feature in six weeks. It proposes to carry out the responsible AI review after launch, based on customer feedback. What should the AI strategist recommend?

- **A.** Integrate the risk assessment and responsible AI requirements into the design and acceptance criteria now, before the build.
- **B.** Accept the plan, because real customer feedback is the most reliable input for a responsible AI review.
- **C.** Postpone the launch by one year so that a complete responsible AI program can be established first.
- **D.** Rely on the foundation model provider's responsible AI practices instead of a company review.

<details><summary>Resposta</summary>

**A.** Governance by design: RAI entra no desenho e nos critérios de aceite, não depois do go-live.

- B: descobrir o problema com o cliente é tarde e caro.
- C: desproporcional; dá para integrar RAI no prazo.
- D: o fornecedor cuida do modelo, mas o uso e o contexto são responsabilidade da empresa.

</details>

### D3-011

`Skill 3.1.2` · Multiple choice

A lender's data science team shows that a complex model is slightly more accurate than the current interpretable model for credit decisions. However, the complex model's decisions are much harder to explain to regulators and to declined applicants. What should the AI strategist recommend?

- **A.** Adopt the complex model, because higher accuracy always creates more business value.
- **B.** Keep the interpretable model permanently, because explainability always outweighs accuracy.
- **C.** Let the data science team make the decision, because it is a technical choice.
- **D.** Make the tradeoff explicit: assess the regulatory explanation requirements and the risk, document the decision, and have the accountable owners approve it.

<details><summary>Resposta</summary>

**D.** Tradeoff entre princípios não tem resposta universal. Depende do risco e da regulação do caso, e precisa ser explícito, documentado e aprovado por quem responde por ele.

- A e B: "sempre" é a marca do distrator; ignoram o contexto.
- C: a decisão tem impacto regulatório e de negócio, não é só técnica.

</details>
